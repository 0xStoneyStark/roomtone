ROOMTONE

ASCII pictures, art-directed by Jev from the music around you.

Not a waveform. The sound around you is put into words — tempo, weight, brightness, rhythm — and Jev, a decision model, answers thirteen questions about them: which picture and what lies beneath it, which marks, which colours, where on the page and how much is left empty, how it should move, and whether the music has turned enough for a new picture. Each scene is rolled from Jev's own probabilities, so the same song never plays the same way twice.
